Admin roles

Owner > Sudo Admin > Admin. Staff can only act on people ranked below them.

What each role can do

Audit log

Bans, role changes, password resets, deletions and permission changes.

Owner controls

Only you can see this. Decide what Sudo Admins and Admins are allowed to do; it applies instantly and is enforced by the database.

Role permissions

You always have every permission.

The rank rule applies on top of every switch: staff can never ban, reset the password of, delete or demote someone at or above their own rank. So admins can never change another admin’s password.
